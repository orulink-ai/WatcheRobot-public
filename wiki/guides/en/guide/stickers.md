# Sticker application guide

Decorate WatcheRobot and its charging dock with the supplied stickers. Follow the 12 illustrated positions for the gray set; the blue set covers 11 positions and skips the top of the body. Click any photo to inspect the pattern, edges and openings at full size.

## What you need {#prepare}

Prepare WatcheRobot, its charging dock and the **four supplied sticker sheets**. Check the materials against the photo, then identify each pattern using the corresponding step.

Turn the robot off and disconnect power from the dock. Keep the shell clean and dry. Support the robot while working; do not force its head or body joints.

![WatcheRobot, charging dock and four supplied sticker sheets](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/preparation.webp)

## Check your sticker color and count {#variants}

| Color | Individual decals | Body-top decal | Sequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gray | 12 | Included | Follow steps 1–12 |
| Blue | 11 | Not included | Go from step 4 to step 6; follow the other steps normally |

Both sets are supplied on four backing sheets. Four refers to the sheets; 12 or 11 refers to the individual decals applied to the device. **The blue set intentionally has no body-top decal. Skip step 5; do not substitute a decal intended for another position.**

Choose the layout for your color and match the **circled number** beside each decal to the step below. Click a layout to enlarge it. The photos show the gray set; the blue set uses the same step numbers but has no ⑤. Do not use decorative numbers printed on the decals (such as “01” or “02”) as step numbers.

## Application technique {#technique}

1. **Align before applying.** Match the pattern, shell edges and openings shown in each photo. Start at one corner and apply slowly, avoiding repeated lifting and repositioning.
2. **Press the pattern down.** Press the patterned area firmly against the shell. Keep screw holes, light-strip openings, connectors and other openings clear.
3. **Wait 5–10 minutes.** Slowly peel away the clear transfer film on the surface, leaving the decorative pattern on the robot.
4. **Stop if the pattern lifts with the film.** Lay the film back down and press that area again. Resume peeling slowly once the pattern adheres; do not pull the pattern off with the film.

::: tip Identifying each position
Left and right labels follow the illustrated sequence. Do not identify a side only by where it appears on screen after rotating the robot: compare the pattern, screw holes and light-strip position in the photo. For every sticker, complete the pressing, waiting and film removal described above.
:::

## 1. Right side of the foot {#step-01}

Find decal ① on your color’s layout. Identify the matching long pattern and align it with the foot's shell edge as shown. Apply it slowly.

![Aligning the right foot sticker along the lower shell edge](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-01.webp)

## 2. Left side of the foot {#step-02}

Find decal ② on your color’s layout. Turn to the other side of the foot. Confirm the pattern's orientation against the photo and follow the shell contour.

![Pattern orientation and placement on the left side of the foot](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-02.webp)

## 3. Front of the foot {#step-03}

Find decal ③ on your color’s layout. Use the front-view photo to align the pattern with the foot contour, then press it down.

![Front foot patterns after application](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-03.webp)

## 4. Left side of the body (the side with screw holes) {#step-04}

Find decal ④ on your color’s layout. Find the side with screw holes. Use the ORULINK lettering and openings in the photo to orient the sticker before applying it.

![Aligning the body sticker on the side with screw holes](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-04.webp)

## 5. Top of the body (gray set only) {#step-05}

**Blue stickers: skip this step and continue to step 6.** The blue set has no body-top decal.

For the gray set, find decal ⑤ and align the light-strip opening first, then follow the top contour slowly. Do not cover the light-strip opening.

![Aligning the top body sticker with the light-strip opening](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-05.webp)

## 6. Right side of the body {#step-06}

Find decal ⑥ on your color’s layout. Use the layout for your color to identify the body-right decal. Check its orientation and the shell boundary, support the robot and apply it. The “02” shown in the gray-set photo is only a visual reference.

![Right body sticker with the 02 pattern](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-06.webp)

## 7. Back of the body {#step-07}

Find decal ⑦ on your color’s layout. Turn to the back. Align the pattern with the shell contour and openings shown in the photo, leaving moving structures clear.

![Rear body sticker aligned with the shell contour](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-07.webp)

## 8. Right side of the face {#step-08}

Find decal ⑧ on your color’s layout. Match the photo to align the edge pattern with the head shell. Keep the sticker off the screen.

![Placement of the pattern on the right face edge](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-08.webp)

## 9. Left side of the face {#step-09}

Find decal ⑨ on your color’s layout. Follow the opposite edge contour and check that the corners adhere.

![Placement of the pattern on the left face edge](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-09.webp)

## 10. Front of the face {#step-10}

Find decal ⑩ on your color’s layout. Use the circular screen edge and top openings to align the front border pattern. Leave the screen, camera and other openings uncovered.

![Completed front face border pattern](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-10.webp)

## 11. Left side of the charging dock {#step-11}

Find decal ⑪ on your color’s layout. Support the dock separately. Align the left pattern with its contour as shown, then press it down.

![Sticker placement on the left side of the charging dock](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-11.webp)

## 12. Right side of the charging dock {#step-12}

Find decal ⑫ on your color’s layout. Use the right-side photo to complete the opposite sticker. Do not cover charging contacts or connectors.

![Sticker placement on the right side of the charging dock](../../../../docs/images/device/stickers/step-12.webp)
